Accepted format

The organisers' case format. Any of these three shapes:

  1. The whole published file — {"cases": [ … ]}. Each case becomes its own dataset with its own ledger and checklist.
  2. One case — {"case_id", "subjects", "compulsory", "students"}.
  3. Just students — {"students": [ … ]}, added to the dataset chosen on the left.
{
  "id": "S001", "name": "Arif Hossain",
  "class": "Class 9", "optional": "HMT",
  "marks": {
    "BAN": 55, "ENG": 61, "MAT": 70,
    "PHY": {"theory": 60, "practical": 20},
    "CHE": {"theory": 40, "practical": 7},
    "BIO": "AB",
    "HMT": {"theory": 50, "practical": 15}
  }
}
  • A subject without a practical part is one whole number, 0–100.
  • A subject with a practical part is {"theory": 0–{{ THEORY_MAX }}, "practical": 0–{{ PRACTICAL_MAX }}}.
  • "AB" means absent. It is graded differently from a mark of 0.
  • Each student has exactly the six compulsory marks plus the mark for their own optional subject (HMT, AGR or REL in the published cases).
  • Students are checked one by one: a bad record is reported with every reason and the rest are still added. Ids must be unique within a dataset.
