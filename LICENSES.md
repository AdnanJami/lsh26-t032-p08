## Data

The student roster and marks in `data.py` are entirely synthetic and
fictional, generated by the team for demonstration purposes. No real
student records, names, or school data were used. First and last names
are drawn from a small hand-picked list of common Bengali given/family
names for realism; they do not refer to real individuals.
